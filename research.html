Masters thesis

Molecular hydrogen in dwarf galaxy simulations

Dwarf galaxies are testbeds for multi-scale galactic events, from single supernovae to whole-halo processes such as ram stripping. This project uses EDGE (Engineering Dwarfs at Galaxy Formation's Edge) data to analyse the distribution of molecular hydrogen in dwarf galaxies.

The aim is to calibrate the $\alpha_{\text{CO}}$ conversion parameter, so that observational campaigns can estimate the molecular hydrogen content of dwarf galaxies more accurately.
