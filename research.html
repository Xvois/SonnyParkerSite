Research

Galaxies, their cores, and the gas between the stars.

I am a computational astrophysics student at the University of Bath, interested in the dynamics of galaxies, their nuclei, and the interactions between them and supermassive black holes.

Masters thesis — MPhys

Molecular hydrogen in dwarf galaxy simulations

Dwarf galaxies are testbeds for multi-scale galactic events, from single supernovae to whole-halo processes such as ram stripping and open-box modelling. Using the EDGE (Engineering Dwarfs at Galaxy Formation's Edge) simulation suite, I am analysing how molecular hydrogen is distributed inside these systems.

The aim is to calibrate the $\alpha_{\text{CO}}$ conversion parameter so that observational campaigns can better estimate the molecular hydrogen content of dwarf galaxies — and to understand the physical processes that drive H2 formation and destruction in these environments.
