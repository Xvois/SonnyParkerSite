EDGE (Masters)

Dwarf galaxies are testbeds for multi-scale galactic events from single stars (SNe) to the entire halo (ram-stripping, open-box modelling). In this Masters project I am using collected EDGE (Engineering Dwarfs at Galaxy Formation's Edge) data to analyse the distribution of molecular hydrogen in dwarf galaxies.

The hope is to calibrate the $\alpha_{\text{CO}}$ conversion parameter to help observational campaigns better estimate the molecular hydrogen content of dwarf galaxies, and to understand the physical processes that drive the formation and destruction of molecular hydrogen in these environments.
